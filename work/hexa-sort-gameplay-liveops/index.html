Game engineering

Hexa Sort: gameplay, LiveOps and monetization

Board mechanics, timed levels and boosters, plus the LiveOps and monetization systems I built into the Hexa Sort Unity client.

Context

Hexa Sort (Lion Studios) is a hexagon-stacking puzzle game for Android and iOS, built in Unity, with 10M+ Google Play downloads.

My role

I was a developer on the Unity game client, building new board mechanics, level features and the LiveOps and monetization systems around them.

What I built

  • I built 10 board mechanics, including Drone, Toaster, Kettle and Generator Nuts.
  • Time-based levels with a Time Freeze booster.
  • Server-timed boosters with Android and iOS local notifications.
  • An in-game mailbox with news cards targeted by app version, level, platform and country, plus remote and bulk gifting.
  • Monetization systems: IAP and restore flows, and rewarded-ad systems.
  • On-device A/B bucketing and Firebase Remote Config kill switches.
