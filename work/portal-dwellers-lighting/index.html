Portal Dwellers: lighting, environments and cutscenes

Real-time HDRP environments I built for Portal Dwellers, with a lighting mood per area, fog and volumetrics, and in-engine cutscenes rendered at 4K.

Context

Portal Dwellers is a dark fantasy, third-person action game built in Unity with the High Definition Render Pipeline (HDRP). Its castles, villages and forests each have their own lighting mood, and the project needed in-engine videos for marketing and client deliverables.

My role

I was a core team member spanning tech art and programming: render-pipeline setup, lighting, profiling and gameplay features.

What I built

  • I built real-time environments in Unity HDRP, combining lighting, post-processing, shaders and VFX.
  • I gave each area its own lighting mood: sunlight through castle windows, torch-lit stone halls, sunlit villages and misty, desaturated forests.
  • I lit the same cutscene by day and by night: hazy daylight in one, darkness lit only by torches in the other.
  • I used fog and volumetrics to soften the distance and give each space depth.
  • I rendered in-engine cutscenes for marketing and client deliverables.
  • I set up the render pipeline and profiled the game, alongside building gameplay features.
