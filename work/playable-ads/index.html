Game engineering

Playable ads in Cocos Creator: Mole and Shoot-to-Sort

Gameplay for two shipped Hexa Sort playable ads, each built around a new mechanic with its own input, animation and level setup.

Context

Playable ads are short interactive ads that a player can try before installing the game. These two promote Hexa Sort, and each showcases a new mechanic: Mole and Shoot-to-Sort.

My role

I built the gameplay for both ads in Cocos Creator, in TypeScript: the input, the animation and the level setup for each mechanic.

What I built

  • I built the gameplay for two shipped playable ads, one for each new mechanic.
  • Mole and Shoot-to-Sort each have their own input handling.
  • Each ad showcases its mechanic with its own animation and level setup.

Stack

  • Cocos Creator 3.x
  • TypeScript
